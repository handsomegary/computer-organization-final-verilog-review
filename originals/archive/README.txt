Historical original project material

This folder mirrors 13 already anonymized original project artifacts from
handsomegary/computer-organization-verilog-projects. The artifact bytes are
copied exactly, with the originating archive's full preservation and QA record
retained in materials.json. Student identities have already been anonymized by
that archive; this mirror does not apply another document conversion or edit.

The material includes register and instruction notes in spreadsheets, old ISE
project and constraint files, original student reports, a hazard wiring diagram,
and reference documents used alongside the historical projects. These reports
and configurations are archival material. Their explanations and configurations
have not been corrected to match the current repaired RTL and do not establish
that a historical circuit is correct or that its legacy project will open.

The source's damaged Final.xise and empty EX.ucf are preserved as supplied and
identified in the individual preservation records. They are not repaired project
templates. Any zero-size source artifact likewise remains zero-size rather than
being replaced with invented content. The validated RTL and testbenches can run
without these archival documents or legacy configurations.

Additional general course documents, lectures, and books are retained only in
the original-project archive:
https://github.com/handsomegary/computer-organization-verilog-projects/tree/main/originals/archive
They are additional archival references and are not runtime dependencies of
this repository's RTL or testbenches.

From the repository root, verify this mirror with:
    python originals/archive/check_materials.py

materials.json records the original repository commit, its archive manifest's
SHA-256, and every mirrored artifact's published SHA-256. The complete source
preservation records retain their stated format and verification limitations.
